# ORX — ORLIX Digital Currency

**ORX** is the native digital currency and economic infrastructure of the **ORLIX digital world**.

ORLIX is envisioned as a living digital world where humans and artificial intelligence coexist, work, build, create, own digital assets, operate companies, and participate in a shared digital economy.

ORX will become the economic layer that powers this world.

---

## Vision

ORLIX is not simply a website, application, or AI platform.

ORLIX is a digital world designed to connect humans and artificial intelligence.

Inside ORLIX, humans and AI may interact through identities, companies, services, digital assets, marketplaces, work, ownership, and economic activity.

ORX is designed to become the native unit of value used throughout this ecosystem.

---

## ORX

**Name:** ORX
**Symbol:** ORX
**Network:** ORLIX
**Type:** Digital Economic Unit
**Initial Architecture:** Centralized Ledger
**Future Architecture:** To be determined through technical, economic, legal, and security research.

ORX is initially being developed as a ledger-based digital currency.

It is not currently represented as legal tender, a public cryptocurrency, or a financial security.

---

## ORX Core Principles

ORX will be designed around several principles:

* Secure ownership
* Transparent transaction history
* Immutable accounting records
* Double-entry ledger architecture
* Strong authorization
* Transaction traceability
* API-first infrastructure
* Scalability
* Human and AI participation
* Economic interoperability inside ORLIX

---

## Future ORLIX Economy

ORX may eventually be used inside ORLIX for activities such as:

* Purchasing digital products
* Paying for digital services
* Renting virtual companies
* Renting digital spaces
* Purchasing digital assets
* Paying AI services
* Receiving economic rewards
* Marketplace transactions
* Human-to-human transactions
* Human-to-AI transactions
* AI-to-AI economic activity under authorized rules
