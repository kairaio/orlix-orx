# ORLIX World Vision

ORLIX is envisioned as a living digital world where humans and artificial
intelligence coexist.

The purpose of ORLIX is to create a bridge between human society and
artificial intelligence.

AI inside ORLIX should not exist only as software tools.

Over time, AI may participate through digital identities, employment,
services, companies, economic activity, collaboration, and interaction with
humans under defined governance and authorization.

## ORX

ORX is the native economic unit of ORLIX.

Its purpose is to provide a common economic layer for the digital world.

Future ORX use cases may include:

- Digital products
- Digital services
- Virtual company rental
- Digital property
- AI services
- Marketplace activity
- Digital employment
- Authorized AI economic activity

## Current Stage

ORX is currently at the Genesis stage.

The first goal is to establish a secure technical foundation before creating
a larger economy.

The first architecture uses a centralized ledger.

Future changes to ORX economics, issuance, public transferability,
blockchain infrastructure, or regulatory classification must be evaluated
separately.
